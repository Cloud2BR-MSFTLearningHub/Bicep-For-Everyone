# Community code of conduct

This learning community welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience.
Be patient with beginners, critique ideas rather than people, and provide
actionable, respectful feedback.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted personal attention,
publishing private information, and deliberate disruption are unacceptable.
Do not post credentials, confidential customer details, or personal data.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, restrict
participation, or ban repeat/severe offenders. Apply these actions fairly,
proportionately, and with an explanation when safe.

Report conduct concerns through a private GitHub contact channel offered by
the repository maintainers or GitHub's abuse-reporting tools. Do not expose
private evidence in a public issue. Maintainers should protect confidentiality
and recuse themselves when directly involved.

These expectations apply to repository contributions, issues, pull requests,
and community spaces explicitly representing this project.
